Metahuman Shader converter for Arnold
Unreal 5.7 / Metahuman for maya plugin



Download & place MHtoArnold folder in
`C:\Users\*username*\Documents\maya\scripts`

1. Open Maya
2. Select the shelf where you want to add button
3. Drag & Drop install_mh_to_arnold.mel

<img  height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9642ab44-6177-4207-95f1-26628718256f" />

In any scene where you imported your metahuman launch script you can convert all shader at once with convert all
You can tweak result (SSS, spec), this also convert wrinkles animated maps.

You can also convert extra shader (multiple eyes & saliva) in MH Extras
